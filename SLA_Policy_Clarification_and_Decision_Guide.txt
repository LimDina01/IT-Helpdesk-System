1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CBVH SCHEDULE ALIGNMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To eliminate unfair SLA breaches and provide true operational performance data for 
management and Power BI tracking, the IT Helpdesk System operates an automated CBVH 
Business Hours Engine tailored to CBVH's jewelry boutique, workshop, and office schedule:

• Working Days: Monday through Saturday (08:00 AM – 05:00 PM).
• Lunch Break: 12:00 PM – 01:00 PM every working day is AUTOMATICALLY FROZEN.
  (Net working time = 8 active hours per day: 4h Morning + 4h Afternoon).
• Weekly Rest Day: Every Sunday (24 hours fully frozen; SLA clock never ticks).
• Public Holidays: Major holidays (Khmer New Year, Pchum Ben, Water Festival, etc.) 
  with HR-decided extensions (+3 to 4 days) are registered in the Holiday Calendar and 
  automatically frozen.
• Overnight Hours: 05:00 PM to 08:00 AM next morning are automatically frozen.
• Zero Manual Interventions: Technicians do NOT need to click manual pause buttons for 
  lunch, evenings, Sundays, or holidays. The system's calculation engine handles this 
  mathematically in real time.

2. THE THREE SLA CLOCKS IN THE SYSTEM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system monitors three distinct phases of a ticket lifecycle:

1. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT SLA
   • Trigger Phase: When Help Desk assigns a ticket to a field technician.
   • Target Window: 30 working minutes (default per category in Admin Settings).
   • Breach Condition: Technician has not clicked "Accept & Start" within 30 working minutes.
   • Business Hours Impact: If assigned at 11:50 AM, the technician has 10 minutes until 12:00 PM, 
     then the clock pauses during lunch (12:00–13:00) and resumes at 1:00 PM with 20 minutes left.

2. RESOLUTION SLA
   • Trigger Phase: Active troubleshooting time until technician marks "Resolved".
   • Target Window: Defined per Category (e.g. 1h, 4h, 8h, 24h, 48h).
   • Breach Condition: Active working hours exceed category target (off-hours, lunch break, 
     Sundays, holidays & daytime vendor holds deducted).

3. AUTO-CLOSE CONFIRMATION SLA
   • Trigger Phase: Once ticket is resolved; waiting for employee confirmation.
   • Target Window: 3 Business Days (72 working hours).
   • Breach Condition: Requester does not confirm or reopen ticket within 3 working days 
     (skips Sundays and official public holidays; auto-closes as "resolved_auto_closed").

3. REAL-LIFE SCENARIOS UNDER THE CBVH ENGINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- SCENARIO A: Saturday 4:30 PM Boutique Ticket Crossing Weekend & Lunch ---
Context: A jewelry boutique barcode printer goes offline Saturday at 4:30 PM (30 minutes before 5:00 PM close).
Category Target: 8 Working Hours. Technician: Dara.

Timeline:
• Saturday 04:30 PM – 05:00 PM: 30 minutes of working time used (7h 30m remaining).
• Saturday 05:00 PM – Monday 08:00 AM: Closed. Off-hours and all of Sunday are AUTOMATICALLY FROZEN.
• Monday 08:00 AM – 12:00 PM: 4 hours of morning working time used (3h 30m remaining).
• Monday 12:00 PM – 01:00 PM: CBVH Lunch Break. SLA clock is AUTOMATICALLY FROZEN (0 minutes counted).
• Monday 01:00 PM – 04:30 PM: 3 hours 30 minutes of afternoon working time used.
• System Target Deadline: Monday 04:30 PM.

-> Result: The ticket is NOT overdue on Monday morning. Dara's SLA metrics are 100% protected.


--- SCENARIO B: Weekday 11:00 AM Ticket Crossing 12:00–1:00 PM Lunch Break ---
Context: Workshop scale calibration software glitch submitted Monday at 11:00 AM.
Category Target: 2 Working Hours (120 active minutes).

Timeline:
• Monday 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM: 1 hour (60 mins) active working time used. (60 mins remaining).
• Monday 12:00 PM – 01:00 PM: Lunch Break. SLA clock pauses automatically.
• Monday 01:00 PM – 02:00 PM: 1 hour (60 mins) active working time used. (0 mins remaining).
• System Target Deadline: Monday 02:00 PM (shifted by 1 hour due to lunch break).

-> Result: Technicians are not penalized for taking their 12:00–1:00 PM lunch break.


--- SCENARIO C: Hardware Failure with Parts Delay (Daytime Operational Hold) ---
Context: Boutique POS terminal requires a replacement motherboard from external supplier.
Category Target: 8 Working Hours.

Timeline:
• Monday 10:00 AM: Created. Technician inspects at 11:00 AM (1 working hour used).
• Monday 11:00 AM: Tech clicks "Mark Blocked" (Reason: Waiting for vendor spare part). SLA clock pauses.
• Tuesday 11:00 AM: Supplier delivers part (8 working hours on hold during business time). Tech clicks "Accept & Resume".
• Tuesday 02:00 PM: Tech completes installation and resolves ticket (2 working hours used, lunch 12–1 skipped).

Calculation:
  Total working time elapsed = 11h.
  Paused working time = 8h.
  Active IT fulfillment time = 11h - 8h = 3 ACTIVE WORKING HOURS.
-> Result: PASSED (3 active hours used vs. 8h allowed). Supplier shipment delay was not charged to technician.

4. MANAGEMENT PERFORMANCE TRACKING & POWER BI INTEGRATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To give management full visibility into individual technician performance and team 
efficiency without requiring complicated manual spreadsheets, the system records 
all SLA metrics ready for Power BI consumption:

Key Data Attributes Provided by the Help Desk Database:
1. Ticket Identification: Ticket Number, Subject, Priority, Status.
2. Classification: Category Name, Issue Type (Boutique POS, Workshop Hardware, ERP/Accounting).
3. Location & Department: Branch/Boutique Name, Department Name, Requester Name.
4. Personnel: Assigned Technician Name, Technician Email, Assignee Role.
5. Lifecycle Timestamps (Cambodia UTC+7):
   • Created At (Submission timestamp)
   • Acknowledged At (Technician acceptance timestamp)
   • First Response At
   • Resolved At (Issue resolution timestamp)
   • Closed At
6. Time Duration Breakdown:
   • Total Wall-Clock Minutes (Full calendar elapsed time)
   • Active SLA Working Minutes (Only Mon–Sat 8–12 & 13–17, excluding lunch, Sundays, and holidays)
   • Paused / On-Hold Minutes (Daytime vendor or parts delays)
   • Acknowledgement Elapsed Minutes
7. SLA Compliance Indicators:
   • Acknowledgement SLA Met (Yes / No: within 30 mins)
   • Resolution SLA Met (Yes / No: within category target)
   • Target Resolution Hours
   • Variance Minutes (Ahead of target vs. Overdue)

Power BI Dashboards Supported:
• Technician Leaderboard: Monthly ticket volume resolved per technician, SLA compliance %.
• Boutique & Branch Heatmap: Volume of IT incidents per store / workshop / head office.
• Vendor / Spare Part Hold Analysis: Bottlenecks caused by external hardware suppliers.
• Peak Hours Analysis: When ticket volume is highest to optimize technician shift coverage.

5. EXECUTIVE DECISION QUESTIONNAIRE FOR MANAGEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Please review and confirm management policy for the following 5 SLA operational decisions:

DECISION 1: CBVH Operating Hours Engine (Mon–Sat 08:00–17:00, Lunch 12:00–13:00 Frozen)
Question: "Do you approve running the automated Business Hours engine (Mon–Sat 8:00–17:00, 
12:00–13:00 lunch break, Sundays, and HR extended holidays frozen)?"

[ ] Option A: Approve CBVH Working Hours Engine [RECOMMENDED]
    SLA counts only Monday–Saturday 08:00–12:00 & 13:00–17:00 (8h/day). Lunch, nights, 
    Sundays, and official holidays freeze automatically.
[ ] Option B: 24/7 Continuous Calendar Clock
    SLA runs continuously through nights, lunch, and weekends (not recommended for jewelry retail).
[ ] Option C: Tiered / Hybrid Clock
    Boutique critical POS alarms run extended hours; back-office tickets run standard hours.

IT Recommendation: Option A (CBVH Working Hours with Lunch Break).


DECISION 2: Resolution SLA Target Driver (Service Category vs. Priority)
Question: "Should target resolution hours be driven primarily by the Service Catalog item 
or by a strict Priority Matrix?"

[ ] Option A: By Service Category (Baseline)
    Each service catalog category defines standard target hours (e.g. POS = 4h, PC = 8h, General = 24h).
[ ] Option B: Standard Priority Matrix
    Strictly determined by urgency level: Critical = 2h, High = 4h, Medium = 8h, Low = 24h.
[ ] Option C: Category Default + Priority Override [RECOMMENDED]
    Category provides baseline, but Help Desk can escalate priority to shorten the SLA window.

IT Recommendation: Option C.


DECISION 3: Technician Acknowledgement Window & Escalation
Question: "How long should a technician have to accept an assigned ticket before alerting supervisors?"

[ ] Option A: 30 Working Minutes Default
    Technician has 30 working minutes to click "Accept & Start".
[ ] Option B: Tiered Acceptance Window [RECOMMENDED]
    15 minutes for Critical boutique incidents, 30 minutes for Normal tickets.
[ ] Option C: Automated Telegram Escalation
    If not accepted within 30 minutes, send an immediate Telegram alert to IT Lead.

IT Recommendation: Option B + Option C.


DECISION 4: Approved Reasons for Daytime Operational Hold (Blocked)
Question: "Which daytime operational reasons are officially approved for placing a ticket on hold?"

[ ] Option A: Three Standard Categories [RECOMMENDED]
    1. Waiting for Spare Parts/Procurement, 2. Waiting for External Vendor (ISP/Software), 
    3. Waiting for Requester Response.
[ ] Option B: Approval Gate Required
    Technician can request a hold, but freeze requires Help Desk Lead approval.
[ ] Option C: Open Technician Discretion
    Technician enters custom reason notes and pauses clock directly.

IT Recommendation: Option A with mandatory reason notes.


DECISION 5: Requester Auto-Closure Window
Question: "Once IT marks an issue resolved, how many business days should the employee have 
to test before automated closure?"

[ ] Option A: 3 Business Days / 72 Hours [RECOMMENDED & INDUSTRY STANDARD]
    Automatically excludes Sundays and public holidays.
[ ] Option B: 2 Business Days / 48 Hours
    Accelerates ticket closure turnaround.
[ ] Option C: 5 Business Days / 1 Week
    Extended buffer for boutique staff on rotating shift schedules.

IT Recommendation: Option A (3 Business Days).
